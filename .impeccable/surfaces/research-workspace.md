## Mode
Operate. The help page is Read.
## Direction contract
User pinned restrained white-and-green, strong contrast, clear type, minimal decoration and professional scientific software. Existing lightweight stack and all scientific/security rules preserved.
## Thesis
A novice completes a real human-reviewed photo measurement using the controls themselves, with recoverable errors and a findable saved result.
## Own-world
White research surfaces, restrained green actions, system sans type, flat sections and explicit scientific units. No marketing metaphor, generic dashboard metrics, decorative imagery or concept comp.
## Story
Choose plant -> photo -> scale when needed -> explicit Analyze -> prominent leaf review -> explicit human approval -> saved result and growth. Existing seven-state model is truth; the compact navigation is secondary to the native action.
## First viewport
Show page purpose, current task, its short instruction and the next real control. A ready photo shows preview/plant/date and one Analyze button. Review puts the photo before the editing controls. Optional details/tips do not obscure errors or required safeguards.
