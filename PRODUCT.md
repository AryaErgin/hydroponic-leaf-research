# Hydroponic Leaf Research
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
First-time students and professors, often on iPhone Safari; returning researchers performing repeated plant measurements. Administrators manage institutional membership.
## Product Purpose
Human-reviewed visible projected leaf-area measurement and growth history. Success is completing a measurement and finding its saved result without external instruction.
## Stack
Existing FastAPI and lightweight HTML/CSS/JavaScript; Firebase Authentication; institution-scoped scientific APIs. No large frontend framework.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve promoted SegFormer, native masks and leaf identities, physical scale safeguards, mandatory human approval, immutable history, tenant isolation and notification/training registries. Pixel-only results must not imply cm2. Keep recently deployed original iPhone photo recovery and format handling. No cloud mutations or deployment in this task.
## Brand Commitments
User confirmed restrained white-and-green, clear typography, strong contrast and minimal decoration. Usability and consistency over aesthetics; professional scientific software, no marketing treatment, generic dashboard or excess cards/gradients.
## Evidence on Hand
Current real integrated UI, automated tests, synthetic procedural image fixtures, previously tested local and deployment evidence. No independent novice participants or physical iPhone available to the agent; never claim those tests.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Aim for WCAG2.2AA where applicable, large touch targets, keyboard focus, announced status and error recovery, 320px through desktop layouts. Existing English UI retained; no translation requested.
